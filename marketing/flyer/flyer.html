How it helps your child

Hands-on, not just tick boxes

Your child drags, builds, measures, colours, sets clocks and sorts shapes.

Learn, practise, earn stars

Everyday objects first, then gradually harder questions. Up to 3 stars for answers right first time.

Kind hints, then “Show me”

Wrong answer? Bolt the robot gives a specific hint. After two tries, “Show me” shows the working.

Follows the school book

Same order as Learner’s Book 3 and Workbook 3 (2nd edition), with matching pages on every activity.

How it helps you

  • Parent corner in every activity: the goal, things to say, mistakes to watch for and a task away from the screen.
  • Dashboard of stars, right-first-time % and the slips to work on.
  • A profile for each child: nickname, animal picture and their own stars.
  • No ads, no tracking or profiling of children. Children never need an email.

Start in 3 steps
