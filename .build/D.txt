Learn screens:
1. "Rows of squares": a 3 × 4 array of squares. Button "Count in rows": each row lights up in turn while the count jumps 4, 8, 12. Show "3 rows of 4 = 4 + 4 + 4 = 3 × 4 = 12".
2. "Turn it round": rotate the rectangle (animated) to 4 rows of 3: still 12. 3 × 4 = 4 × 3.
3. "Build it": a grid with a rectangle anchored at the top-left; the child drags the bottom-right corner handle (snaps to grid lines) — also provide + / − buttons for rows and columns for easy laptop use. Live label "rows × columns = area" e.g. "2 × 3 = 6". Task: make area 6. lockNext when area is 6 (any of 1×6, 2×3, 3×2, 6×1).
Practice (count 8):
- Level 1: show an array (rows 2–5, columns 2, 3, 4, 5 or 10). "How many squares?" number input; hint shows counting in rows (repeated addition); reveal animates the row jumps. Mistake 'Counted only one row'.
- Level 2: "Build a rectangle with area 12" (then 8, 10, 15, 18, 20) with the drag/± builder; accept any factor pair that fits the grid; praise names other possible rectangles (e.g. "Other ways: 1 × 12, 2 × 6, 3 × 4").
